PRIVACY

Your files stay
in your browser.

Glyphora is designed to process document contents on your device. It does not provide user accounts, a document upload API, a database, or cloud document storage.

What happens when you select a file?

Your browser reads the file locally. Image OCR and scanned-PDF OCR run using Tesseract.js in your browser. Digital PDF text is read with PDF.js, and DOCX text is extracted with Mammoth. Extracted text is held in the current page session until you close or refresh the page.

Third-party assets

For the initial release, JavaScript libraries and OCR language data are loaded from third-party content delivery networks when needed. The font may also be loaded from Google Fonts. These services may receive ordinary connection metadata such as your IP address and browser request information. The document contents are not intentionally sent to these services by Glyphora. A future self-hosted build can bundle these assets with the website.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to register or sign in.
  • We do not upload or store your selected documents.
  • We do not maintain a document history or user dashboard.
  • We do not send extracted text to an AI API.

Your responsibility

Use a trusted device and browser. Avoid browser extensions you do not trust. When handling sensitive documents, remember that browser memory, device backups, screenshots and downloaded exports are outside Glyphora's control.

Review and adapt this privacy notice to your final deployment and any hosting, CDN or analytics configuration.
