ABOUT THE PROJECT

Documents, made
more accessible.

Glyphora is a lightweight, browser-based document text extraction tool. It is designed to help people turn supported scans, images and documents into editable text without creating an account or sending document contents to a Glyphora server.

How it works

Glyphora uses open-source browser libraries. Tesseract.js performs optical character recognition, PDF.js reads and renders PDF pages, and Mammoth extracts text from DOCX files. Processing happens in your browser after the required application libraries and language data have loaded.

Built with privacy in mind

There is no Glyphora account system, document database, upload endpoint or cloud document history. Your selected files are handled locally by the browser session.

Recognition has limits

OCR is not perfect. Results can vary with scan quality, handwriting, page layout, language, fonts and image resolution. Always review important names, numbers, dates and legal or financial information.
